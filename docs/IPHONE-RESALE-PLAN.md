# iPhone resale launch — October 7, 2026

Research, not measured conversion analytics. No access to verified traffic or sales data was used.

Swappa listings disclose model, storage, condition, carrier and often battery health: https://swappa.com/en/listings/apple-iphone-15?condition=good
Back Market markets a one-year seller limited warranty: https://help.backmarket.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010237699-What-do-I-need-to-know-about-the-Protection-Plan-and-1-year-warranty
Gazelle separates returns, condition and optional protection information: https://buy.gazelle.com/pages/help-center

Recommendation: compete on actual-device transparency, local conversation and clear terms. Do not advertise the strongest warranty: proposed 3–4 months is shorter than Back Market’s published year. Warranty scope/duration and returns remain owner decisions; no final policy published.

Live-ready inquiry section avoids fake stock, prices or refurbished/certified claims. Before adding purchasable listings obtain actual photos, model/storage/color, condition, battery health, carrier lock, owner-confirmed lawful ownership/activation-lock clearance, tested functions, accessories, cost, selling price and exact warranty/return terms. Do not publish IMEI/serial numbers.

Pricing method: collect at least five comparable current listings per exact model/storage/carrier/condition; use median asking price as a benchmark, not sale-price evidence. Subtract acquisition, repairs, fees and warranty reserve to evaluate margin. No defensible price until inventory and costs are supplied.

Measurement plan: weekly sales-section views, inquiry clicks, qualified inquiries, quotes, completed sales and gross margin. Calculate inquiry rate = inquiries/views and quote-to-sale = sales/quotes. No tracking pixels or third-party analytics enabled in this change.
